Jupiter

Jupiter is the Fifth planet from the Sun in our solar system.

- Gas giant (mostly hydrogen and helium) - Largest planet in our solar system - Diameter: about 142,984 km (88,846 miles) - Mass: 318 times that of Earth! - Great Red Spot (a giant storm) - 79 known moons (Gymmeade, Europa, Io, Callisto...) - Rotates really fast (9.9 hours) - Jupiter's magnetic field is MASSIVE

Jupiter facts

  • Diameter: 142,984 km (88,846 miles)
  • Mass: 1.898 × 10^27 kilograms
  • Moons: 79
  • Oribit distance: 778.5 million kilometers (483.8 million miles) from the Sun
  • oRBIT period: 11.86 Earth years
  • Surface temperature: -145°C (-233°F)
  • First record: 1600 BCE
  • Recorded by: Babylonians, and later, ancient Greek astronomers
