Solar system

Mars

Mars is the 4th planet from the Sun in our solar system.

Mars is the red planet! - 4th planet from the Sun - Rocky planet with a thin atmosphere - Home to the tallest volcano (Olympus Mons) and largest canyon (Valles Marineris) - Has polar ice caps and possible water evidence - Curiosity Rover's been exploring it since 2012!

Mars facts

  • Diameter: 6,794 kilometers (4,220 miles)
  • Mass: 6.4171 x 10^23 kg
  • Moons: 2
  • Oribit distance: 227.9 million km (141.6 million miles)
  • oRBIT period: 687 Earth days
  • Surface temperature: -67°C (-89°F)
  • First record: 1600 BCE
  • Recorded by: Ancient Egyptians and Babylonians
