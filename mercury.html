Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the sun and due to its proximity it is not eaily seen except during twillight

For every two orbits of the sun Mercury completes three rotations about its axis and up until 1965 it was thought that the same side of Mercury constanstly faced the sun. Thirteen times a centuary Mercury can be observed from the earth passing across the face of the sun in an event called transit.
