Uranus

Uranus is the 7th planet from the Sun in our solar system.

Uranus is the sideways planet! - 7th planet from the Sun - Tilted on its side (98° axis) - Ice giant with methane clouds - 27 moons, including Miranda - Voyager 2 flew by in 1986

Uranus facts

  • Diameter: 51,118 km (31,763 miles)
  • Mass: 8.681 × 10^25 kg
  • Moons: 27
  • Oribit distance: 2.9 billion km (1.8 billion miles)
  • oRBIT period: 84 Earth years
  • Surface temperature: -195°C (-320°F)
  • First record: 1781
  • Recorded by: William Hersche
