Neptune

Neptune is the 8th planet from the Sun in our solar system.

Neptune's the farthest planet! - 8th planet from the Sun - Ice giant with strong winds - Blue color from methane gas - 14 moons, including Triton (with g gutters!) - Voyager 2 visited in 1989

Neptune facts

  • Diameter: 49,528 km (30,775 miles)
  • Mass: 1.024 × 10^26 kg
  • Moons: 14
  • Oribit distance: 4.5 billion km (2.8 billion miles)
  • oRBIT period: 165 Earth years
  • Surface temperature: -353°F (-214°C)
  • First record: 1846
  • Recorded by: Johann Galle and Heinrich d'Arrest
