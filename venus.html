venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun in our solar system.

- Second planet from the Sun - Hottest planet (average temperature: 462°C/863°F) - Similar size to Earth (diameter: 12,104 km/7,521 miles) - Thick atmosphere, mostly CO2, creating a strong greenhouse effect - Slow rotation period: 243 Earth days - No moons - Often visible in the sky as the "Morning Star" or "Evening Star"

venus facts

  • Diameter: 12,104 kilometers (7,521 miles)
  • Mass: 4.867 × 10^24 kilograms (about 81.5% of Earth's mass)
  • Moons: None
  • Oribit distance: 108 million kilometers (67 million miles) from the Sun
  • oRBIT period: 225 Earth days
  • Surface temperature: 462°C (863°F)
  • First record: 1600 BCE
  • Recorded by: Babylonian astronomers
