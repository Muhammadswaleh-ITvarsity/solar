Sun

The closest planet to the sun is mercury

The Sun is a massive ball of hot, glowing gas, primarily composed of hydrogen and helium. It's about 4.6 billion years old and is expected to live another 5 billion years. Surface temperature: around 5,500°C (10,000°F). Core temperature: scorching 15,000,000°C (27,000,000°F). The Sun is about 149.6 million km (92.96 million miles) away from Earth. Its gravity holds our solar system together. It's a massive source of energy, powering life on Earth through photosynthesis. Solar flares and sunspots can affect Earth's magnetic field and cause aurorae. The Sun is a relatively small star compared to others in the universe
